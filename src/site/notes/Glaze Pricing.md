# Clear Oxidation Glaze Cost Analysis

A concise breakdown of three **simple**, **stable**, and **affordable** clear glaze recipes for electric-kiln oxidation firing at 1120 °C, 1220 °C, and 1280 °C. Calculations use Ceramique Industries prices, Ceramique 1110 as the 3134 frit substitute, and Mumbai residential electricity (₹ 9.81 / kWh).

---

## 📋 Assumptions & Parameters

- **Kiln draw**: 6.5 kW  
- **Firing durations**:  
  - 1120 °C (Cone 2–3): 6 h  
  - 1220 °C (Cone 6): 10 h  
  - 1280 °C (Cone 8): 12 h  
- **Electricity rate**: ₹ 9.81 / kWh  
- **Load**: 40 mugs (5″ × 5″), glaze use ≈ 0.1515 kg per mug  
- **Frit substitute**: Ceramique 1110 (₹ 255 / kg)

---

## 🔧 Recipes & Material Costs

| Temp      | Ingredients per 1 kg batch                                                                                  | Material Cost (₹/kg) |
|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| 1120 °C   | 200g Potash Feldspar<br>200g Silica<br>200g Whiting<br>200g EPK Kaolin<br>200g Ceramique 1110 frit         | 94.00                |
| 1220 °C   | 300g Nepheline Syenite<br>240g Colemanite (Gerstley Borate)<br>200g Silica<br>165g EPK Kaolin<br>95g Wollastonite<br>20g Talc | 95.65                |
| 1280 °C   | 400g Potash Feldspar<br>150g Whiting<br>330g Silica<br>50g EPK Kaolin<br>40g Zinc Oxide<br>30g Dolomite    | 64.75                |


---

## ⚡ Electricity Cost per Mug

| Temp     | Firing Energy Cost (₹)           | €/mug (₹)       |
|----------|----------------------------------|---------------:|
| 1120 °C  | 6.5 kW × 6 h × 9.81 = ₹ 382.59   | 382.59 / 40 = **9.56**  |
| 1220 °C  | 6.5 kW × 10 h × 9.81 = ₹ 637.65  | 637.65 / 40 = **15.94** |
| 1280 °C  | 6.5 kW × 12 h × 9.81 = ₹ 765.18  | 765.18 / 40 = **19.13** |

---

## 🗒 Notes & Recommendations

- **Most affordable overall**: 1120 °C at **₹ 23.82/mug**  
- **Best balance**: 1220 °C yields durable glass with moderate cost (₹ 30.44/mug)  
- **High-fire trade-off**: 1280 °C uses cheapest materials but incurs higher energy (₹ 28.94/mug)  
- **Ceramique 1110 frit** matches Ferro 3134 chemistry (high-calcium borosilicate, low alumina)—1:1 substitution  
- Always **test** glazes on sample pieces to confirm fit and surface quality  
- **Record** specific gravity (1.45–1.48) and firing curves for reproducibility  
- Adjust batch sizes or combine loads to optimize kiln efficiency
