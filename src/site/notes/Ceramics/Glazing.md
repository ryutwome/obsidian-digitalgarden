# Oxidation Clear Glaze Cost Analysis
---

## 📋 Assumptions & Parameters

- **Kiln draw**: 12 kW  
- **Firing durations**:  
  - 1120 °C (Cone 2–3): 8 h  
  - 1220 °C (Cone 6): 12 h  
  - 1280 °C (Cone 8): 14 h  
- **Electricity rate**: ₹ 12 / kWh  
- **Load**: 40 mugs (5″ × 5″), glaze use ≈ 150g per mug  
- **Frit 3134 substitute**: Ceramique 1110 (₹ 255 / kg)

---

## 🔧 Recipes & Material Costs

| Temp    | Ingredients                                                                                                 | Total Material Cost (₹/kg) |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1120 °C | 50 Gerstley Borate  <br>30 China Clay  <br>20 Silica                                                        | 90.00                      |
| 1220 °C | 20 Wollastonite  <br>20 Ceramique 1110 frit (3134) <br>20 Potash Feldspar  <br>20 Silica  <br>20 China Clay | 100.00                     |
| 1280 °C | 40 Soda Feldspar  <br>30 Silica  <br>20 Whiting   <br>10 China Clay                                         | 52.00                      |

---

---

## 💰 Total Cost per Mug

| Temp    | Material per Mug (₹) | Firing per Mug (₹) | **Total per Mug (₹)** |
| ------- | -------------------: | -----------------: | --------------------: |
| 1120 °C |                   14 |                 41 |                    55 |
| 1220 °C |                   15 |                 62 |                    77 |
| 1280 °C |                    8 |                 76 |                    84 |


*Material per mug = Material cost per kg × 150gm glaze per mug*

---

## 🗒 Notes & Recommendations

- **Most affordable overall**: 1120 °C at **₹ 55/mug**  
- **Best balance**: 1220 °C yields durable glass with moderate cost (₹ 77/mug)  
- **High-fire trade-off**: 1280 °C uses cheapest materials but incurs higher energy (₹ 84/mug)  
- **Ceramique 1110 frit** matches Ferro 3134 chemistry (high-calcium borosilicate, low alumina)—1:1 substitution
