## Porcelain

### Standard
🔥 Cone 8-11
- 25-50 China Clay
- 0-25 Ball Clay (Replace some of all of ball clay for whiteness at the cost of reduced workablity)
- 25 Feldspar
- 25 Silica
- 2-4 Bentonite (Increases plasticity but also increases drying shrinkage)

### Standard Translucent 
🔥 Cone 6-8
- 20-40 China Clay
- 0-20 Ball Clay (Replace some of all of ball clay for whiteness at the cost of reduced workablity)
- 35-40 Feldspar
- 20-25 Silica (Better glaze fit at the cost of translucency)
- 2-4 Bentonite (Increases plasticity but also increases drying shrinkage)

### Tom Coleman Mid Fire
🔥 Cone 6-8
- 30 Grolleg Kaolin
- 30 Silica
- 25 Nepheline Syenite 
- 15 Ball Clay
- 2 Bentonite

## Stoneware

### Goshen College
🔥 Cone 8-11
- 40 Ball Clay
- 30 Fire Clay
- 20 Feldspar
- 10 Silica
- 2 Bentonite
