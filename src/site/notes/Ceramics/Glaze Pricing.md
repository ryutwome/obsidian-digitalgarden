# Oxidation Clear Glaze Cost Analysis
---

## 📋 Assumptions & Parameters

- **Kiln draw**: 12 kW  
- **Firing durations**:  
  - 1120 °C (Cone 2–3): 8 h  
  - 1220 °C (Cone 6): 12 h  
  - 1280 °C (Cone 8): 14 h  
- **Electricity rate**: ₹ 12 / kWh  
- **Load**: 40 mugs (5″ × 5″), glaze use ≈ 150g per mug  
- **Frit 3134 substitute**: Ceramique 1110 (₹ 255 / kg)

---

---

## 🗒 Notes & Recommendations

- **Most affordable overall**: 1120 °C at **₹ 55/mug**  
- **Best balance**: 1220 °C yields durable glass with moderate cost (₹ 77/mug)  
- **High-fire trade-off**: 1280 °C uses cheapest materials but incurs higher energy (₹ 84/mug)  
- **Ceramique 1110 frit** matches Ferro 3134 chemistry (high-calcium borosilicate, low alumina)—1:1 substitution
